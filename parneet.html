Sp. 06 Retrospectio Reflection

Looking Back

If you were to give advice to your first-year self, what would it be?

Don't be afraid to dive into challenging projects early on — the best learning happens when you're slightly uncomfortable. Build things that interest you, even if they seem ambitious, because practical experience solidifies classroom concepts better than anything else. Also, embrace the community around you: join clubs, attend tech meetups, and collaborate with peers. The connections you make and the teamwork skills you develop are just as valuable as the code you write. Finally, remember that it's okay to ask for help and that every expert was once a beginner.
