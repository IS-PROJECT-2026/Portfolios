Looking Back

Advice I would give myself at the start of university.

Start early, build consistently, and document everything.

If I were to give advice to my first-year self, what would it be?

I would tell my first-year self to focus on fundamentals, practise coding consistently, and not wait for everything to feel perfect before building projects. I would also document my work earlier, ask questions sooner, and treat every small project as a chance to improve my confidence and problem-solving skills.
